When setting up a functional workspace in a small bedroom, studio apartment, or shared dorm, storage is almost always the main bottleneck. Loose paperwork, charging cables, extra hardware, and daily office supplies quickly take over a compact desktop if they do not have a dedicated home. 

Two of the most popular modular storage solutions for home offices are the IKEA ALEX Drawer unit and the Bisley 5-Drawer Steel Cabinet. Both are widely praised in the home office community for keeping clutter out of sight, but they take fundamentally different approaches to construction, footprint, and daily utility
