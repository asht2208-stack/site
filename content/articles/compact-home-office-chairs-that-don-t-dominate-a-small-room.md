When setting up a home office in a studio apartment, a small bedroom corner, or a shared living room, large executive task chairs quickly become visual and physical obstacles. Standard ergonomic chairs often feature massive five-star bases, thick padding, and fixed high backs that swallow up tight floor space and ruin the aesthetic of a residential room.

To keep a small workspace functional and open, you need a compact office chair designed specifically for tight footprints. In this guide, we break down what to look for when choosing a space-saving chair that delivers necessary support without taking over your living area.
