When setting up a workspace in a small bedroom, studio apartment, or dorm room, visual clutter directly impacts how spacious the room feels. Cables hanging loosely beneath a compact desk create visual noise, catch on your feet, and make a tight room feel chaotic.

Two primary solutions dominate cable management for home offices: under-desk cable trays and adhesive cable raceways. While both aim to eliminate tangles, they serve very different structural functions. Note that because specific hardware recommendations depend heavily on your exact desk dimensions and surface material, none of the individual products from our standard hardware catalog fit as direct picks for this guide. Instead, this article breaks down how trays and raceways function so you can select the right approach for your compact layout.
