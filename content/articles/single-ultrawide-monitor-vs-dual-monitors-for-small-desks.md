    *   **Which Should You Choose? Decision Framework:**
        *   Choose Ultrawide if: Desk is under 40 inches wide, you hate center bezels, you prioritize clean cable management, or you mostly work in split-screen productivity.
        *   Choose Dual Monitors if: Desk is 44+ inches wide, you share your screen constantly on video calls, or you need a vertical/portrait monitor orientation.
    *   **Maximizing Space on a Small Desk:**
        *   Use monitor arms (like a heavy-duty arm or single arm) to reclaim surface area under the display.
        *   Mount power strips behind or under the desk surface.
