When setting up a home office in a tight space—whether it is an apartment corner, a small bedroom nook, or a college dorm room in the USA—the single biggest challenge is preserving desktop real estate. Once you place a laptop or monitor, a keyboard, and a notepad on a compact desk, you are often left with zero surface area for anything else. 

The most effective solution for small-footprint offices is moving storage off the desktop and onto the wall. Vertical storage lets you keep everyday accessories within arm's reach without cluttering your main workspace. Two of the most popular vertical systems on the market today are pegboards and metal wall grid organizers.
