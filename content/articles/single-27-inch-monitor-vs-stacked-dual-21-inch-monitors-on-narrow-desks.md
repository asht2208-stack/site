Setting up an efficient workstation in a small apartment, dorm room, or bedroom corner usually means working with a narrow desk. When your desktop depth is restricted to 20 inches or less, traditional side-by-side dual monitors quickly become impractical. They overhang the edges of the desk, force uncomfortable neck rotation, and swallow up every square inch of usable work
