Living in a compact urban studio, shared apartment, or dorm room often means every square foot has to work double duty. When your bedroom doubles as your primary workspace, leaving a large desk out 24/7 can quickly make a small room feel cramped and cluttered. Foldable desks offer an appealing solution: set up a functional workstation during work hours, then collapse and store it away when you need your floor space
