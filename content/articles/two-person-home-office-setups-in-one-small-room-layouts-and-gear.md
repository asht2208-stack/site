Setting up a dual home office in a small US apartment, dorm, or shared bedroom requires careful planning. When two people share a compact room, floor space vanishes quickly. Choosing equipment with low physical footprints—such as chairs with flip-up arms, compact standing desks, monitor mounts, and flat-plug power strips—allows both partners to work comfortably without crowding the room.
