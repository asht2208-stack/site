## Desk Lighting in Tight Spaces

When you are working with a small desk or squeezing a work-from-home setup into a bedroom corner, traditional desk lamps are luxury items you simply cannot afford. A standard lamp base swallows several square inches of precious surface area—space that should be reserved for your keyboard, mouse pad, or notepad. 

Monitor light bars solve this exact footprint crisis by mounting directly to the top frame of your display, projecting downward task light across your workspace without touching the desk surface.
